Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Applies to the TakeOne app for Android.

Short version: Your audio never leaves your device. TakeOne does not have accounts, servers, or analytics SDKs that upload recordings, scripts, or file metadata. Crash diagnostics stay on the device unless you choose to share them. The app shows ads only in the free tier; Pro customers see no ads.

1. Audio, scripts and files

  • Recordings, edits and exports never leave your device. All processing — recording, DSP, time stretch/pitch, noise removal, compression, normalization, conversion and export — runs locally.
  • Records are stored only in the app's private storage (scoped storage on Android 10+). Uninstalling the app removes them, exactly like any other file your device deletes.
  • Teleprompter scripts you write are stored locally and are never uploaded, backed up to Google Auto Backup, or transmitted anywhere.
  • Exports (m4a/mp3/wav/flac/opus/amr/wav32) are saved to the folder you pick via the system file picker, or shared through the app you choose. TakeOne does not receive copies of exported files.
  • Auto Backup: TakeOne excludes all take files and library metadata from Android Auto Backup and device transfer; restoring a backup will not restore your recordings. It will never include your audio.

2. Microphone and storage access

  • Microphone — used strictly to record takes. No audio is ever streamed, transmitted, or processed off-device, and never while paused or between takes. You can revoke the permission at any time in system settings.
  • Storage / SAF — used for importing files you explicitly pick, and for saving or sharing files you explicitly ask to export.
  • No camera, no precise location, no contacts, no SMS, no call logs, no accessibility services, no background location — ever.

3. Advertising

TakeOne contains ads provided by Google AdMob; ad requests are mediated by Google on your behalf. Ads are never shown while recording or while audio plays.

  • EEA / UK / Switzerland — a Google-certified consent form (UMP) is shown on first run; ads are personalised only with your consent, otherwise they are non-personalised.
  • Other regions — Google may use your device's advertising ID to serve and measure ads. You can reset or limit ad tracking in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Pro customers see no ads at all once entitled. (Free tier shows one post-action interstitial per completed save/export/share and one banner/card at most.)
Google's ad technology may also process device data (such as the advertising identifier) to personalise, measure and report ads. See Google's Ads Privacy and your system's advertising settings to limit that.

4. In-app purchases and billing

  • TakeOne Pro is a subscription/one-time purchase processed by Google Play Billing. TakeOne's servers never see your payment method; Google handles payment and entitlement resolution.
  • The app stores only an “entitled” flag (and the SKU) in local preferences to remember Pro status; it is re-checked against Google Play whenever the app can reach it. It is never uploaded elsewhere.
  • No free-tier trial abuse: trial eligibility is managed by Google Play on a per-Google-account basis.

5. Diagnostics and crash logs

  • Crash stack traces and a small set of “breadcrumbs” are kept on your device. They are never uploaded automatically.
  • If a crash happens, the app may offer to share the log; the share sheet always shows you the full contents first, paths are redacted, and breadcrumbs are capped at 64 KB and overwritten on the next crash.
  • You may export-or-delete crash data from the app at any time.

6. Data practices in one table

  • Uploaded audio / recordings: never.
  • Analytics SDKs (Firebase, Mixpanel, Amplitude…): none bundled.
  • Crash reporting SDKs: none; crash logs are on-device only.
  • Advertising: Google AdMob (ads only in free tier), with UMP consent.
  • Billing: Google Play Billing only.
  • Android Auto Backup: all take files, library DB and scripts excluded.

7. Children

TakeOne is not directed at children. No content is targeted at users under 13 (or under the applicable age of consent).

8. Your choices

  • Revoke mic permission any time: Settings → Apps → TakeOne → Permissions.
  • Reset ad ID / limit ad personalization: Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the app or clear its storage in system settings — takes, scripts, preferences and crash logs are deleted.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date at the top of this page changes too, and significant changes will be announced in the app's release notes.

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact: support@takeone.app
