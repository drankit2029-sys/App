TakeOne is a complete voice-and-music studio that fits in your pocket — not a
pocket-sized imitation of one.

RECORD & MASTER
• 32-bit float recording with true-peak limiting, so quiet performances stay
  intact and loud ones never clip.
• Pro noise suppression, de-esser, compressor, EQ, reverb, chorus and more —
  all processed on-device, in the rack you already know.
• Loudness normalization to broadcast targets (EBU R128) and one-tap mastering
  presets.

EDIT LIKE AN EDITOR
• Non-destructive punch-in edits on the waveform: select, trim, delete,
  correct pitch and speed, or splice takes.
• Undo everything. Your takes are never altered invisibly.

TELEPROMPTER BUILT IN
• A proper scrolling teleprompter with mirror mode for hardware mirrors,
  word-count and reading-time estimates, and adjustable speed and size.

LIBRARY & EXPORT
• Organize, favourite, search and filter takes; import audio; export to
  WAV32/WAV24/WAV16, FLAC, M4A, Opus or AMR; share straight from the app.

PRIVACY FIRST
• Audio never leaves your device. No cloud uploads, no accounts, no trackers.
  Ads are never shown during recording or playback.
• Free tier: supported by thoughtful, interstitial-free-after-action ads.
  Pro unlocks everything, forever, once.
