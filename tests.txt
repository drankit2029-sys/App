14. AMR SPEED AUDIT (user question: is fast AMR a bug?)
- Not a bug. AMR is the only format that decimates before encoding: 48kHz
  -> 8kHz (AMR-NB, factor 6) or 16kHz (AMR-WB, factor 3), so one sixth or
  one third of the samples ever reach the encoder; it also encodes mono at
  12.2/23.85 kbps against 128 kbps for M4A. Less work, less data.
- Verified it is not skipping work. FormatSpeedAuditTest converts the same
  10s 48kHz source to every format and checks the ENCODED FILE, not the UI:
  decoded duration within 9.4-10.9s, byte count within +-35% of bitrate x
  duration, and the AMR re-imported to a real signal. Passes.
- Worth knowing: an AMR save is a mono downmix, and the decimator assumes a
  48kHz source. Every raw in this app is 48kHz (recorder and importer both
  resample to it), so that assumption currently holds.
